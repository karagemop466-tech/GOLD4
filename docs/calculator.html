Formula (definitions, not price claims): Pure gold (g) = weight (g) × karat/24 · Melt value = pure gold (g) ÷ 31.1034768 g per troy oz × spot price. The troy-ounce constant is the NIST definition (NIST SP 811). You enter today's spot yourself from an official source (LBMA / Kitco — links on Sources) so no stale or invented price is baked into this site.
