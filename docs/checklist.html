Before you buy

#CheckWhat "good" looks likeRed flag
1Gold purity (karat)10K / 14K / 18K stated. Higher karat = more gold (14K = 58.3%, 18K = 75%).Karat not stated
2Solid goldListing explicitly says solid goldPlated, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay, gold-tone
3Hallmark10K/14K/18K stamp plus maker's/manufacturer's markStamp only, or no stamp
4WeightWeight in grams provided on requestSeller won't provide weight
5Seller reputationIndependent reviews/complaints checked; real contact infoNo address/phone, only glowing on-site reviews
6Price sanityCompare price with gram weight × current gold value (calculator)Unusually cheap vs. melt value
7Return policyReturnable after inspection; check exclusions for resized/custom ringsAll sales final / custom excluded
8PaymentCredit card (purchase protection)Wire, gift cards, irreversible methods
9DocumentationSave listing, receipt, description, purity claimsVerbal-only claims

Upon arrival

  • Before wearing/resizing: take to an independent jeweler; verify purity + weight.
  • Best verification: XRF testing or another professional purity test — don't rely solely on the stamp.
  • If the seller won't let you independently verify/return it: don't buy it.
