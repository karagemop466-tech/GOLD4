Solid gold vs. look-alikes (per the buying checklist)

  • Solid gold — the whole piece is gold alloy of the stated karat. Listings must say solid gold explicitly.
  • Gold plated / gold-tone / overlay — a thin surface layer over base metal. Not what this project tracks.
  • Vermeil — gold plating over sterling silver. Still plating.
  • Gold-filled — a thicker bonded gold layer (US trade usage commonly cites ≥1/20th of weight); still not solid.
Definitions above are general trade usage for orientation. For the binding US labeling rules see the FTC Jewelry Guides (link for manual review; page not fetched at build time): ftc.gov — Jewelry Guides.

Hallmarks

A karat stamp (10K/14K/18K or 417/585/750) plus a maker's/manufacturer's mark is what the checklist asks for. A stamp alone does not prove purity — verify with an independent jeweler and, ideally, XRF testing after delivery (see Checklist). No per-item hallmark data is published on the retailer pages we fetched, so those cells are blank + flagged, not guessed.
