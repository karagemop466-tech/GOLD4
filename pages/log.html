⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

No hallucinations. This project manufactures no values. A price, weight, karat, deadline or contact detail is only shown if it was read from the official page linked in the same row. The consumer rules being applied come from the FTC's guidance on buying gold jewelry and 15 U.S.C. § 295.

  • Blank means blank. If a retailer did not publish a gram weight, the weight cell stays empty — it is never estimated from photos, blogs or "typical" rings.
  • Every row carries its source. Open the source link and confirm the number yourself; prices change without notice.
  • Derived ≠ claimed. Gold content and gold value are arithmetic on published values at the captured spot price, and are labelled as derived.
  • Anything uncertain is published on Flagged for Review with the reason and the source, instead of being quietly filled in.

Pass 2 — dataset assembly + first integrity audit PASS after fixes

2026-10-22T23:17Z · data/*.json, scripts/compute.py, scripts/audit.py

  • Karat fineness table = karat ÷ 24, cross-checked against the FTC karat definition
  • Legal minimum fineness per stamp = fineness − 0.007 (15 U.S.C. § 295)
  • Derived gold grams, gold value, legal-minimum value and price/gold multiple recomputed independently by the audit script
  • Phantom-math check: no derived value may exist for a row without a published weight
  • Blank-value checks: blank price requires PRICE_UNVERIFIED, blank weight requires NO_WEIGHT_PUBLISHED or WEIGHT_UNVERIFIED
  • Domain allow-list: every source URL must belong to the named retailer or a government/benchmark host
  • Banned-domain scan: no marketplace, resale, blog or social domain anywhere in the data
  • Speculation-language scan on all free-text fields
  • Two real inconsistencies found on Blue Nile's own pages (18k copy on a 14K page; white-gold copy on a yellow-gold page) and one mixed-metal ambiguity (platinum prongs on a 14K ring) — published on the Flagged page instead of being smoothed over

Outcome: Two audit bugs were found and fixed (fineness rounding tolerance, derived-value rounding). The audit now re-derives every number and fails on any mismatch.
