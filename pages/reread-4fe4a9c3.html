Re-verification after 19 days: what changed, and what could not be re-read.

The prices on this site were read on 22 and 23 October 2026. On 11 November a GitHub runner re-opened all 64 ring pages and this session hand-read 16 of them. 56 prices were read again; 29 of those differ from October. 7 rows now show a blank price. The gold benchmark moved +1.58%.

⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

No hallucinations. Policy: never manufacture values. A price, weight, karat, deadline or contact detail is only shown if it was read from the official page linked in the same row. The consumer rules being applied come from the FTC's guidance on buying gold jewelry and 15 U.S.C. § 295.

  • Blank means blank. If a retailer did not publish a gram weight, the weight cell stays empty — it is never estimated from photos, blogs or "typical" rings.
  • Every row carries its source. Open the source link and confirm the number yourself; prices change without notice.
  • Derived ≠ claimed. Gold content and gold value are arithmetic on published values at the captured spot price, and are labelled as derived.
  • Anything uncertain is published on Flagged for Review with the reason and the source, instead of being quietly filled in.
  • Re-run the check after every review. python3 scripts/audit.py re-derives every number and fails the build on an unsourced value. When you review this dataset, ask of each row: where is the official source, and what date was it read? If a row cannot answer that, treat it as unverified and flag it — including anything added by a previous pass.

What the rules were

A price was updated only where that day's evidence printed the current amount. The earlier value is kept in the row's history. A page that is gone or sold out gets a blank price, never a carried-over one. A row nobody could read keeps its October date and a flag. No number was estimated, averaged or converted.

The update is done by scripts/session_4fe4a9c3/apply_reread.py, which copies values from the two evidence files and types none of its own.

R11-02 · The automated price check would have confirmed 5 stale prices HIGH

On 5 Ross-Simons pages the visible text prints the sale price while the page's machine-readable data still carries the pre-sale amount. The repo's rule marks a price CONFIRMED when the machine-readable amount equals the stored one, so a check on 11 November would have reported the October prices as confirmed although the page displayed lower ones. A CONFIRMED state is therefore evidence about the page's metadata, not about the price a shopper sees.

Rows / files: RS-01, RS-02, RS-03, RS-04, RS-05. Each was compared with the visible page text.

Before buying

Open the official page and read the price for the exact size, metal and stone you want. Ask the seller in writing for the gram weight, the karat stamp and maker's mark, and whether a resized ring can be returned. Pay by credit card and keep the listing and receipt. On arrival, have an independent jeweller weigh it and test the purity before you wear or resize it. A 14K or 18K stamp alone does not prove purity.

Recommended after every review: look for hallucinations. Pick rows at random, open the official link, and compare each number and its date with this page.
