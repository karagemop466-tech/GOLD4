Flagged for Review.

Everything this project could not confirm from an official source, every contradiction found on a retailer's own page, and every policy clause that can cost you money. These items do not block publication — they block a purchase until you check them.

⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

No hallucinations. This project manufactures no values. A price, weight, karat, deadline or contact detail is only shown if it was read from the official page linked in the same row. The consumer rules being applied come from the FTC's guidance on buying gold jewelry and 15 U.S.C. § 295.

  • Blank means blank. If a retailer did not publish a gram weight, the weight cell stays empty — it is never estimated from photos, blogs or "typical" rings.
  • Every row carries its source. Open the source link and confirm the number yourself; prices change without notice.
  • Derived ≠ claimed. Gold content and gold value are arithmetic on published values at the captured spot price, and are labelled as derived.
  • Anything uncertain is published on Flagged for Review with the reason and the source, instead of being quietly filled in.
  • Re-run the check after every review. python3 scripts/audit.py re-derives every number and fails the build on an unsourced value. When you review this dataset, ask of each row: where is the official source, and what date was it read? If a row cannot answer that, treat it as unverified and flag it — including anything added by a previous pass.

On the 14K Comfort Fit 2.5 mm page the description says the ring is "crafted in 18k yellow gold" while the specification block says 14K Yellow Gold. On the 14K Riviera Pavé page the description says the diamonds are "set in 14k white gold" while the title says 14K Yellow Gold.

A "14K" ring with platinum prongs

The Classic Four Prong Solitaire description mentions platinum prongs while the title and specification list only 14K Yellow Gold. If the prongs are platinum, the gold weight is lower than the ring weight, so the gold value calculation would overstate the gold content.

Recorded on BN-07.

Weights labelled "approximate"

Allurez publishes "Approximate Metal Weight", Anjolee publishes "Approx. Metal Weight", and VRAI publishes a metal weight for ring size 6. None is a measurement of the ring you will receive; a size change moves the weight. Every derived gold value inherits that uncertainty.

Mejuri's price depends on which colourway the page opens on

The same Mejuri product URL opens on whichever metal the site selects. The Diamonds Team Ring and the Pavé Diamond Slim Signet opened on white gold, and the Ziggy Ring opened on sterling silver at "From $398" — the 14k yellow gold variant in this dataset reads "From $598". A saved link can therefore show a different price than the row you are comparing. Select the metal and stone explicitly before you read the price.

Recorded on MJ-01, MJ-03 and MJ-05. The yellow-gold variants were opened separately on 2026-10-22 to confirm the recorded prices.

Brilliant Earth's resize terms changed between captures of the same page

The Atelier Solitaire page showed "Free 1-Year Resizing" on the re-read of 2026-10-22, while the earlier capture of the same page described one complimentary resize within 60 days of purchase. Both were read from Brilliant Earth's own page, so the seller is the only source that can settle which applies to your order — and it matters, because the resize has to happen without destroying the return right.

Recorded on the Sellers page for Brilliant Earth, with the page linked.

The gold spot panel moved during the day while its date stayed put

The World Gold Council page showed $0.00/oz on a second read on 2026-10-22, against the frozen $4,028.53/oz used for every calculation here, while the page's date line still read "Data as of 21 October, 2026". A live panel is not a fixed benchmark. This project keeps the first read so no derived number silently restates itself, and publishes the second read beside it.

Both reads are recorded in data/gold_spot.json; source: World Gold Council.

No seller publishes a hallmark

Zero of 47 rows show the physical stamp or the maker's mark, which is the one item on the checklist that can only be verified in your hand. The FTC guidance is blunt: if the karat mark has no trademark beside it, don't buy it.

FTC 16 CFR § 23.3(c) cautionary case: "hollowed 14k solid gold"

Mejuri's Bold Chain Ring gallery alt-text states "handcrafted in hollowed 14k solid gold" while the variant selector says 10k Yellow Gold ($348) and Specifications lists "Hollowed 14K gold". Under 16 CFR § 23.3(c), hollow items must not be described as "solid". Excluded from canonical solid rows and documented in IRR-CAT-10.

Brilliant Earth's BE-04 displays a "Free 1-Year Resizing" badge above copy stating resizing is unavailable, while BE-03 pairs an 18K shank with 14K white gold prongs. Blue Nile's BN-12 says 18K Yellow Gold (1/6 ct) in the title vs "platinum" and "1/8 ct" in details. Mejuri's MJ-06 states 0.532 ct in prose vs 0.044–0.056 ct in specs. Basis of the "legal minimum" arithmetic (for review)

The legal-floor column computes karat ÷ 24 minus the 0.007 whole-article tolerance of 15 U.S.C. § 295. Under the millesimal convention a 14K article is often assayed against 585 parts per 1000 instead of 583.3; the same tolerance on 585 would give 57.8% rather than 57.63%. Both readings are published in data/methodology.json so the basis is explicit. Neither figure measures a particular ring — only an assay does.

Brilliant Earth prices render in the visitor's currency

During the pass-7 re-check the 14K Atelier page served a EUR rendering ("1,350.00 EUR (Setting Only)") with a Global-e checkout while the sibling 18K page served USD. The recorded USD figures come from same-day USD captures, and the 18K figure re-confirmed exactly, but the 14K figure could not be reproduced in the non-USD rendering. Flagged on BE-01 and BE-02: open the page with United States / USD selected and re-read the price before relying on it.

What would change my mind

A gram weight per size
