R8-SOLID-01
Needs review		A solid-status flag is not a substitute for explicit solid wording

Both canonical rows were marked stated, but their recorded solid_gold_statement reads Pure 24k Gold sold by weight with Certificate of Authenticity. That recorded quotation does not explicitly say solid. The readiness register withholds solid-wording credit rather than inferring it from pure/24K, a certificate, a product name or a boolean. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the items are plated or hollow. Canonical correction was requested from the active integration owner.

R8-RETURNS-02
Needs review		Seven days does not establish an unconditional cash-refund right

The reviewed Menē terms distinguish merchandise credit and original-payment refunds, provide for conditional restocking/assessment fees, and exclude engraved/customized/personalized discretionary returns. The inherited seller exclusions do not enumerate all these clauses. A generic window cannot establish whether independent inspection/testing of a particular configured ring preserves a refund right. Checks that cannot be completed by a product listing

Before paying

Require the exact metal, size, weight scope and price; explicit solid-gold wording; karat and maker-mark evidence; independent seller research; written return eligibility including resized/custom exclusions and inspection/testing permission. Prefer a credit card, not an irreversible payment. Preserve the listing and all written claims.

After delivery, before wearing or resizing

Arrange an independent professional check of weight, markings, construction and purity within the applicable return window. Ask the professional which tests are appropriate. Obtain the seller’s written permission before any test that may alter the ring.
