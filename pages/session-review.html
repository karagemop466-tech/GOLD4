Before any purchase

Require an explicit solid-gold description, karat, gram weight and weight scope, maker mark plus hallmark, written return and independent-testing permission (including resize/custom exclusions), and a current configuration-specific price. Keep the listing and receipt; prefer a credit card. Before wearing or resizing, have an independent jeweler check weight and purity with an appropriate professional test, such as XRF with its limitations explained. If independent verification and a suitable return are not allowed, do not buy.

The calculator uses a dated benchmark, not a live bullion price or a resale quote. Gross ring weight must not be passed off as metal-only weight when stones or other materials are included.
