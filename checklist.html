12-Point Solid Gold Engagement Ring Checklist & Seller Script

Complete all 12 steps in sequence — from pre-checkout written gram-weight confirmation and credit card payment through immediate 48-hour post-arrival jeweler inspection and multi-spot XRF testing.

0 / 12 checks completed

Verify exact numeric karat fineness in both product title AND specification table. Under 16 CFR § 23.3, anything under 10K cannot legally be sold as karat gold in the U.S.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Caught TWO live listings on Sarraf (`SKU F1625` at $20,671 and `SKU 121987-.72P` at $2,858) where H1 title karat contradicts spec table karat (`18K` vs `14K`)! · Verify Primary Source ↗

Confirm the piece is solid karat gold throughout — NOT hollow, gold-plated, gold vermeil (>=2.5um gold over 925 silver), gold-filled, rolled gold plate, gold overlay, or gold-tone.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Caught Mejuri's `Organic Dome Nesting Ring Set` ($188) inside `/collections/diamond-rings`, whose copy literally contains the substring `'18k solid gold'` inside `'thick layer of 18k solid gold plated on 91% recycled sterling silver'`! · Verify Primary Source ↗

Under the National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. § 297), any karat stamp (`14K`, `585`, `18K`, `750`) MUST be accompanied by a registered manufacturer/seller trademark stamp (`MEJURI`, `MENE`, etc.). Request macro photos of inner-shank stamps.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Across all 21 solid-gold online listings audited, almost no retailer displays macro hallmark photography online — and GIA documented fake `14K` disks glued inside non-solid jewelry. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Get net precious-metal gram weight in writing for the exact finger size ordered before checkout. Deduct gemstone weight (`1.00 ct = 0.200 g`) when computing pure gold mass.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Brilliant Earth, Blue Nile, Catbird, and Mejuri omit static gram weights on engagement settings, while Sarraf quotes wedding band weights at men's Size 10 (~12-18% heavier than women's Size 6). · Verify Primary Source ↗

Check whether prongs/basket use a different alloy than the shank (e.g., 14K white gold or platinum head on an 18K yellow gold band, or rhodium electroplating on white gold per 16 CFR §§ 23.7–23.8).

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Caught Brilliant Earth `BE1199H6-18KY` (18K band + 14K white gold prongs) and Blue Nile `500000y` (18K band + Platinum prongs) advertised under single-karat titles. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Compute `Fine Gold (g) = Net Metal Weight (g) x (Karat / 24)` and multiply by dated spot gold (`$129.39/g` Kitco on `2026-10-22`). Plain machine-made solid bands cluster near `1.33x-1.50x` melt; any ring priced near or below `1.0x` melt is almost certainly hollow or plated.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Immediately flags $188 vermeil dual-band set as physically impossible for solid 18K gold (`5g` of 18K melt alone = `$485.23`), and exposes extreme designer markups. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Confirm a real verifiable street address, telephone/email customer service, independent reviews/complaints history, and transparent corporate identity before submitting payment.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Verified physical headquarters and published support channels across all 6 audited retailers on `sellers.html`. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Confirm exact return window length, whether the clock starts at SHIPMENT or DELIVERY, restocking/shipping fees, missing lab certificate penalties, and custom/engraved/resized final-sale exclusions.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Menē gives only **7 days**; Catbird gives **20 days** (`$9` fee, international final sale); Blue Nile starts its 30-day clock on **SHIPMENT date** (`$150` missing cert fee); Sarraf voids warranty upon third-party resizing and charges `max($100, 10% of price)` for warranty replacement. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Order raw, UNENGRAVED, standard stock size first so the ring remains 100% refundable during independent testing. If a seller refuses returns after non-destructive inspection, DO NOT BUY.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Engraving or resizing at checkout immediately converts otherwise refundable rings at Blue Nile, Catbird, and custom settings at Brilliant Earth into non-returnable or fee-penalized orders. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Use a major credit card — never wire transfer, ACH, Zelle, debit card, cash app, or cryptocurrency — to retain statutory dispute rights under the Fair Credit Billing Act (60-day written dispute window).

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Protects full purchase amount if assay or gram weight contradicts written product claims and seller stalls refund. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Save full-page timestamped PDF/screenshots of the product page, live chat/email gram-weight confirmation, invoice, and GIA/LBMA certificates before shipping.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Retailer prices and specs drift rapidly (e.g., Brilliant Earth setting jumped `$650 -> $850` between search cache and `2026-10-22` live page). · Verify Primary Source ↗

Within 48 hours of delivery (TAGS INTACT, UNWORN, BEFORE RESIZING!), have an independent local graduate gemologist/jeweler: (1) weigh on calibrated 0.01g scale, (2) inspect hallmarks, prongs & seams under binocular microscope, and (3) run multi-spot EDXRF metal purity analysis.

Real Trap Caught in 2026-10-22 Audit: Defeats single-point XRF spoofing from thick electroforming over resin/wax or 18K gold plated over 10K alloy documented by GIA Carlsbad Laboratory. · Verify Primary Source ↗

Copy-Paste Written Inquiry Script (Send Before Paying)

Subject: Written Spec Confirmation Request Before Order — [SKU / Ring Name, Size X]

Hello, before I place my credit card order for [Ring Name / SKU] in US Ring Size [X] (unengraved), please confirm the following in writing by email:
1. Exact solid karat gold fineness of BOTH the band/shank AND the prong head (confirming 100% solid karat gold throughout — not hollow, vermeil, gold-filled, or plated).
2. Expected net precious-metal weight in grams (excluding gemstones) for US Size [X], plus manufacturing tolerance.
3. Exact quality hallmark and registered maker's trademark stamped inside the shank.
4. Confirmation that an unworn, unengraved ring in Size [X] may be inspected non-destructively (0.01g scale, microscope, and XRF) upon delivery and returned for a 100% full refund within [X] days if any specification differs from the above.

Thank you!
