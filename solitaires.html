CATEGORY 01 · SOLITAIRE ENGAGEMENT RINGS & MOUNTINGS

Verified Solitaire Engagement Rings & Settings (8 Official Listings)

Side-by-side comparison of 14K and 18K solitaire diamond rings and mountings across Sarraf, Brilliant Earth, Blue Nile, and Catbird NYC — separating setting-only mountings ($850–$1,390) from complete diamond solitaires ($2,183–$4,068).

Multi-Metal Prong Heads Disclosed in Fine Print

Brilliant Earth BE1199H6-18KY ($850) pairs an 18K yellow shank with 14K white gold prongs, while Blue Nile #500000y ($1,125) pairs an 18K yellow shank with Platinum prongs. Platinum prongs add durability; 14K prongs reduce total fine-gold purity slightly and read 14K on prong XRF!

Women's vs. Men's Weight Disclosure Asymmetry

None of the 6 women's solitaire listings disclose static gram weight online (all 6 kept strictly UNVERIFIED), whereas Sarraf's heavy men's solitaires publish exact weights (8.00 g 14K = $603.84 melt; 6.30 g 18K = $611.39 melt).

Minimum Band Thickness Warning (1.0mm–1.5mm)

Ultra-whisper shanks like Catbird's 1.0mm Swan Solitaire ($3,900) and Brilliant Earth's 1.5mm Petite Setting ($850) carry minimal gold mass and higher bending/warping risk over decades of daily wear than 1.9mm–2.2mm+ bands.
