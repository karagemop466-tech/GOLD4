6. Limits — read these before trusting this page

  • Spot-check, not a sweep. Seven live product URLs were re-read; 57 rows were not.
  • Blue Nile serves a soft 404 — HTTP 200 carrying 404 copy. A status-code-only check would call those pages alive.
  • The scanners reconcile numbers against stored evidence, and stored evidence can itself be stale: today's WGC panel is +1.58 % from the stored benchmark, so a page that matches data/ perfectly can still be 19 days behind the market.
  • Passing gates are not proof. The eight legacy pages passed every gate while printing a superseded benchmark, because no gate compared a rendered legacy number against data/gold_spot.json. That hole is what check_superseded.py now closes; it is offered to the final integration as a candidate Gate 10.
  • Corroborating a stored price today does not authenticate the ring.
Standing rule, restated: after every review, look for hallucinations again — source link, capture date, read path, and whether anything was inferred rather than read.
