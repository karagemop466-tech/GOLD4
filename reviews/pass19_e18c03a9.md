## ⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

> **No hallucinations.** Every price, weight, karat, policy and link below was read from the official page linked beside it during this pass, or is explicitly labelled as a *stored/historical* value with its capture date. If a value could not be confirmed from an official source it is left **blank** and flagged `UNVERIFIED`. Nothing is estimated, averaged, inferred from photographs, converted between currencies, or copied from a blog, marketplace or price-comparison site. Derived numbers (pure-gold grams, gold value, price ÷ gold, percentage differences) are arithmetic on published inputs at the frozen, dated spot price — and are labelled as derived. **Re-run the hallucination passes after every review** (`scripts/audit.py`, `scripts/audit_hallucinations.py`, and the other gates in `.github/workflows/pages.yml`) and ask of each value: *where is the official source, and what date was it read?*
>
> A 14K or 18K stamp by itself does **not** prove purity. The safe sequence remains: reputable seller + karat mark **plus** maker's/registered-trademark mark + published gram weight + price that makes sense against the gold content + credit-card payment + keep all documentation + **independent jeweler verification (XRF or equivalent) before wearing or resizing**. If a seller will not let you independently verify and return the ring, do not buy it.

## Buying checklist — where each requirement stands

| # | Requirement | Status in this dataset (with evidence) |

| 4 | Weight in grams | **17/64 publish it**; 47 stay blank by design. Ask the seller in writing before buying any blank-weight row |
| 5 | Seller reputation + real contact | Contact routes captured per seller on the [Sellers page](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/GOLD4/pages/sellers.html); reputation/complaints **not** verified — search independently (FTC step 2) |
| 6 | Price vs gold weight/value | Computed only for the 17 weighted rows (§ calculator). An unusually cheap price vs gold value is a red flag; MSRP anchors are not evidence |
| 7 | Return policy (resized/custom exclusions) | Quoted per seller; resized/custom/engraved exclusions are common — **verify purity before resizing**. Get the policy in writing |
| 8 | Credit-card payment | Recommended (federal billing-error dispute rights); avoid irreversible methods. See [Method](https://karagemop466-tech.github.io/GOLD4/pages/methodology.html) |
| 9 | Documentation | Every row keeps its official source URL + capture date; keep listing, receipt and purity claims with your order records |
| 10 | Independent verification on arrival (XRF) | Required before wearing/resizing. A stamp alone proves nothing; § 295 allows 7‰ under the mark whole-article — only testing settles it |
| — | Seller won't allow verify/return | **Don't buy it.** No row in this dataset is a purchase approval |
