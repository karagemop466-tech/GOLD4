6. What this page does not claim

  • It does not re-read any of the 64 product pages, prices, weights, karats or availability — that is the concurrent Pass 21 owner's scope.
  • It does not establish that any ring is solid gold, hallmarked, or worth its price. A stamp is not a test; only an independent professional check (ideally XRF) answers that, after delivery.
  • It does not re-fetch 16 CFR Part 23, NIST, CFPB or the remaining USC pages.
  • The policies quoted are promises read on 2026-11-11, not terms this project can enforce — which is why every row carries its URL and its read date.
