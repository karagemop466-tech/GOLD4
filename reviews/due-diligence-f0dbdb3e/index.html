Method, limits and how to re-check

How each value was recorded

  • Browser-rendered page reader. The sandbox has no general outbound internet from the shell, so a script cannot re-fetch these pages; the stored quotations are the evidence.
  • read_utc is the sandbox clock reading taken immediately before the batch of page reads that contained the page.
  • Quotations are the reader's text with line breaks collapsed. Where the reader ran two adjacent page elements together without a space (for example 'complaintsin' or 'Average of26'), the single missing space is restored. An ellipsis (…) marks omitted text between two parts of the same page. No word or digit is added, changed or reordered.

What this register does not establish

  • A BBB grade is BBB's opinion and covers about three years; it is not a finding about any ring, and BBB says it does not verify what third parties tell it.
  • Complaint counts are not adjusted for company size. A large retailer will have more complaints than a small one selling the same quality.
  • Only the first part of the longer BBB complaint and review lists was read: the summary counts and the most recent entries. Older entries were not read.
  • Seller pages were read through a browser-rendered reader at the times shown. A page can change the next day.
  • Where a page would not render or a statement was not found, the cell is blank and flagged. Nothing was filled in from memory, from a search summary or by inference.
  • No Trustpilot, Reddit, state attorney-general or court record was used as a source of values. No US state business registry was opened.
  • Nothing here establishes that any ring is solid gold, correctly stamped or worth its price. That still takes a scale, a loupe and a jeweler's test on arrival.

Checks run on this page

  • Every value sits next to the words the page printed, and every figure in a value occurs in those words.
  • A blank is blank and carries a flag.
  • Seller facts come from that seller's own domain, reputation facts from bbb.org, registry facts from a regulator.
  • Every calculation re-derives exactly; every count in the prose is recomputed.
  • No figure appears on this page that is absent from the evidence file; every table row has an official https link.
  • The page loads no script, form, image or remote resource, and equals a fresh render of the evidence file.
