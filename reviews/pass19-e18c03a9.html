6 · Buying checklist (apply before any purchase)

  1. Purity: confirm 10K/14K/18K in the spec field — then verify the stamp on arrival.
  2. Solid gold: listing must say solid gold — reject plated, vermeil, filled, overlay, gold-tone for this brief.
  3. Hallmark: karat stamp plus maker's/registered-trademark mark (0/64 publish it — inspect on delivery).
  4. Weight: demand grams in writing (only 17/64 publish it).
  5. Seller: independent complaints/reviews + real contact details.
  6. Price sanity: run §1 with today's spot; unusually cheap vs gold value = red flag.
  7. Returns: window + resized/custom/engraved exclusions in writing — verify before resizing.
  8. Payment: credit card; avoid irreversible methods.
  9. Records: save listing, receipt, description, purity claims.
  10. On arrival: independent jeweler + XRF/professional assay before wearing/resizing. No verify/return → no buy.
