⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

No hallucinations. This project manufactures no values. A price, weight, karat, deadline or contact detail is only shown if it was read from the official page linked in the same row. The consumer rules being applied come from the FTC's guidance on buying gold jewelry and 15 U.S.C. § 295.

  • Blank means blank. If a retailer did not publish a gram weight, the weight cell stays empty — it is never estimated from photos, blogs or "typical" rings.
  • Every row carries its source. Open the source link and confirm the number yourself; prices change without notice.
  • Derived ≠ claimed. Gold content and gold value are arithmetic on published values at the captured spot price, and are labelled as derived.
  • Anything uncertain is published on Flagged for Review with the reason and the source, instead of being quietly filled in.
  • Re-run the check after every review. python3 scripts/audit.py re-derives every number and fails the build on an unsourced value. When you review this dataset, ask of each row: where is the official source, and what date was it read? If a row cannot answer that, treat it as unverified and flag it — including anything added by a previous pass.

What the data actually shows

Only 17 of 64 rows can be priced against their gold at all

Four Allurez settings, six Sarraf bands/solitaires, four Ross-Simons bands, two VRAI settings and one Anjolee ring publish a gram weight (17 rows total; Sarraf plain bands set a 1.33×–1.50× melt floor, while AN-01/SR-05/SR-06 include centre diamonds). Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth, Catbird and Mejuri publish prices without gram weights, so their gold content stays blank — not guessed.

Price ÷ gold-value multiple across rows with both a price and a published weight: 1.33× to 6.65×. Compare Ross-Simons 18K 0.7 g band (RS-02, 0.525 g pure Au) vs 14K 1.7 g band (RS-01, 0.992 g pure Au): higher karat with lower gram weight yields 47% less fine gold. Also see Assay Lab+ → and Stone-Deduction Calculator →

18K costs more than its extra gold — and by an inconsistent amount

Same design, same retailer, only the karat changed: Petite Solitaire is +15.5% going 14K→18K, Brilliant Earth Atelier is +16.8%, Classic Four-Prong is +29.3%, Comfort Fit is +40.1%, and Allurez Split Shank is +68.0%.

For the Allurez pair both prices and weights are published, so the 18K premium can be split: +54.9% of it is simply more gold, and the rest is labour and margin.

See the full karat analysis →

Zero retailers publish the physical hallmark

Not one product page in this dataset shows the inner-shank stamp or registered maker's mark, which is why the checklist requires post-delivery inspection. Under 15 U.S.C. § 297(b) a karat mark must be accompanied by a registered trademark or legal company name, and GIA Lab Notes advise pairing XRF with gram-weight checks.

Return windows range from 7 to 30 days — and resizing kills returns

Menē allows 7 days (10% cash-refund restocking fee), Catbird allows 14–20 days ($9 fee; special orders final sale), and Blue Nile's 30-day clock starts at shipment. Brilliant Earth, Blue Nile, Ross-Simons, VRAI and Allurez exclude resized rings — verify purity before resizing.

How to use this

1 · Check the row

Open the source link and confirm the price is still what the row says. Prices move; this dataset is a snapshot, not a feed.

2 · Ask for the weight

If the weight cell is blank, ask the seller in writing for the gram weight of the exact ring size you are ordering. Refusal is an answer.

3 · Verify on arrival

Independent jeweller, XRF or assay, before you wear or resize it. A 14K stamp is a claim with a legal tolerance, not a measurement.

No-hallucination policy: every price, weight, policy and link on this site was read from the official page named beside it on 22 October 2026. Where a value could not be read, the cell is blank and the row is on the Flagged for Review page.

Gold benchmark: $4,028.53/oz as of 21 October, 2026 (World Gold Council) = $129.5202/g. Derived values are arithmetic on retailer-published inputs, not appraisals.

This site is research, not financial, legal or gemmological advice. Verify any ring in person with an independent jeweller, including XRF or assay, before relying on its purity.
