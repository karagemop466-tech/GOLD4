⚠ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read before using any number

No hallucinations. This project manufactures no values. A price, weight, karat, deadline or contact detail is only shown if it was read from the official page linked in the same row. The consumer rules being applied come from the FTC's guidance on buying gold jewelry and 15 U.S.C. § 295.

  • Blank means blank. If a retailer did not publish a gram weight, the weight cell stays empty — it is never estimated from photos, blogs or "typical" rings.
  • Every row carries its source. Open the source link and confirm the number yourself; prices change without notice.
  • Derived ≠ claimed. Gold content and gold value are arithmetic on published values at the captured spot price, and are labelled as derived.
  • Anything uncertain is published on Flagged for Review with the reason and the source, instead of being quietly filled in.

What the data actually shows

Only 6 of 20 rows can be priced against their gold at all

Four Allurez settings and two VRAI settings publish a gram weight, so only those rows have a computable gold content. Blue Nile and Mejuri publish a price but no weight, so their gold content is blank — not guessed.

Price ÷ gold-value multiple across the six computable rows: 2.17× to 3.73×.

18K costs more than its extra gold — and by an inconsistent amount

Same design, same retailer, only the karat changed: Petite Solitaire is +15.5% going 14K→18K, the Comfort Fit is +40.1%, and the Allurez Split Shank is +68.0%.

For the Allurez pair both prices and weights are published, so the 18K premium can be split: +54.9% of it is simply more gold, and the rest is labour and margin.

See the full karat analysis →

Zero retailers publish the hallmark

Not one product page in this dataset shows the physical stamp or the maker's mark, which is exactly why the checklist says to verify on arrival. The legal requirement (karat mark plus a registered trademark) is quoted on the Method page.

Return windows all say 30 days — the exclusions differ

Every seller here offers ~30 days, but Blue Nile, VRAI and Allurez exclude resized or altered rings, Mejuri excludes engraving outright, and Allurez excludes custom orders while Anjolee explicitly allows returns on customised orders. Read the clause, not the badge.

How to use this

1 · Check the row

Open the source link and confirm the price is still what the row says. Prices move; this dataset is a snapshot, not a feed.

2 · Ask for the weight

If the weight cell is blank, ask the seller in writing for the gram weight of the exact ring size you are ordering. Refusal is an answer.

3 · Verify on arrival

Independent jeweller, XRF or assay, before you wear or resize it. A 14K stamp is a claim with a legal tolerance, not a measurement.

No-hallucination policy: every price, weight, policy and link on this site was read from the official page named beside it on 22 October 2026. Where a value could not be read, the cell is blank and the row is on the Flagged for Review page.

Gold benchmark: $4,024.36/oz as of 21 October, 2026 (World Gold Council) = $129.3862/g. Derived values are arithmetic on retailer-published inputs, not appraisals.

This site is research, not financial, legal or gemmological advice. Verify any ring in person with an independent jeweller, including XRF or assay, before relying on its purity.
