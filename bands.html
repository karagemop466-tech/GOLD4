Solid Gold Engagement Bands, Eternity Rings & 24K Rings (9 Official Listings + Disqualified Control)

Plain solid gold bands with documented weights provide the cleanest empirical benchmark in jewelry economics: at $4,024.60/oz spot gold, real solid gold bands cluster tightly between 1.33× and 1.50× raw gold melt.

Size-10 Reference Weight Adjustment Guide (Why Finger Size Changes Melt Value)

Retailers that publish band gram weights (such as Sarraf's 4.80g / 6.70g / 9.40g / 11.10g plain bands) publish the weight for a standard men's Size 10 ring (~19.8mm inner diameter = ~62.1mm inner circumference), whereas a women's Size 6 ring (~16.5mm inner diameter = ~51.9mm circumference) has ~15–17% shorter circumference while sold at the flat Size-4–8 retail price! Always ask for exact weight at your ring size before computing exact markup.
