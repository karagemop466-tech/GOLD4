U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) · FTC Consumer Guidance

FTC Consumer Advice — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry ↗

  • Defines 24-karat purity scale (14K = 14 parts gold, 10 parts alloy) and requires trademark stamp beside any quality mark.
  • Distinguishes solid karat gold from hollow gold and from plated, gold-filled, rolled gold plate, gold overlay, and vermeil (gold over sterling silver).
  • Advises buyers to get karat weight, gram weight, and return policy in writing before purchasing.

Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (NARA / FTC) · 16 CFR §§ 23.1–23.18 (incl. § 23.3 Gold, § 23.4 Vermeil, § 23.7 Rhodium, § 23.8 Multi-Metal, § 23.9 Quality Marks)

16 CFR Part 23 — Guides for the Jewelry, Precious Metals, and Pewter Industries ↗

  • 16 CFR § 23.3: Unqualified 'Gold' means 24K through and through; <10K cannot be called karat gold; hollow centers must not be deceptively described.
  • 16 CFR § 23.4: Vermeil requires sterling silver (>=92.5% Ag) coated with >=2.5 microns of >=10K gold — never solid gold.
  • 16 CFR § 23.7 & § 23.8: White gold rhodium plating and multi-metal components (e.g., 18K shank + platinum/14K prongs) require non-deceptive disclosure in weight order.
  • 16 CFR § 23.9 & 15 U.S.C. § 297 (National Stamping Act): Every quality stamp must be accompanied by the registered manufacturer/seller trademark.
Live Verified: 2026-10-22 · https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-23
Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) · 15 U.S.C. § 1666 / Regulation Z (12 CFR § 1026.13)

CFPB Official Guidance — Disputing Credit Card Charges (Fair Credit Billing Act) ↗

  • Pay exclusively by credit card (never wire, Zelle, crypto, or debit): preserves statutory billing-error and goods-not-as-described chargeback protections.
  • Written dispute notice must reach card issuer within 60 days after the first statement showing the charge; creditor must acknowledge within 30 days and resolve within 2 billing cycles (max 90 days).

Gemological Institute of America (GIA) · Gems & Gemology, Winter 2024

GIA Gems & Gemology — Chemical Analysis in the Gemological Laboratory (EDXRF) ↗

  • Energy-dispersive X-ray fluorescence (EDXRF) provides rapid, non-destructive elemental analysis from Na to U but measures only the shallow near-surface excitation depth.
  • Because XRF is surface-shallow, heavy plating or gold-on-gold plating can skew single-spot surface readings unless paired with mass/density and microscopic inspection.
Live Verified: 2026-10-22 · https://www.gia.edu/gems-gemology/winter-2024-chemical-analysis
Gemological Institute of America (GIA Carlsbad Laboratory) · Gems & Gemology, Spring 2024, Vol. 60, No. 1

GIA Lab Notes — Electroformed Gold Piece with Adhered 14K Hallmark Fraud Case ↗

  • Item submitted as '14K solid gold' had a glued-on 14K hallmark disk over a wax/resin core coated with up to ~200 microns of electroformed gold.
  • Standalone surface XRF read 17.99K and 22.47K gold without detecting subsurface core! Fraud was caught by suspiciously low weight-in-hand + binocular microscope examination.
  • Proves why buyers MUST combine calibrated gram scale weight + microscopic hallmark/seam inspection + multi-spot XRF.
